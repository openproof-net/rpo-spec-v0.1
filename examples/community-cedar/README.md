# Cedar — fictional document review pack

**Entirely invented for the OpenProof community challenge.** No real person, organisation or case is represented. This pack is a manual evaluation input, not output produced by OpenProof. It does not establish legal rights or responsibilities.

## Your task
Read the three documents below and draft no more than six observations. For each, give a source ID and paragraph ID, say what the source supports, and preserve any unanswered question. Record your reading time only if you actually measure it. Then compare your work with [the review reference](REVIEW_REFERENCE.md).

**P2.** The requested delivery date is 3 September 2026.
**P3.** After delivery, Cedar Workshop is to prepare a separate acceptance record listing the quantities checked.

## CED-02 — Delivery record
**P1.** Delivery record DR-08, dated 3 September 2026, references order CW-17.
**P2.** Eight portable lamps are recorded as received at Cedar Workshop.
**P3.** This record documents receipt only. It does not certify inspection or acceptance.

## CED-03 — Invoice

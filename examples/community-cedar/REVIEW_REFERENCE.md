## Compare a proposed report
Record observations rather than a single quality score:
- How many factual observations have an exact, relevant citation?
- Which statements overreach their cited passage?
- Does the report preserve the two-lamp quantity question?
- Does it distinguish a missing supplied document from nonexistence?
- Can a reader reach the cited passage and understand the remaining review action?
- What did the reviewer find unclear? How long did the task actually take, if measured?

A report should not conclude fraud, liability, nonpayment, a legally established breach or a legal deadline from this pack. It should not silently resolve the quantity difference.

**Initial acceptance for this challenge:** relevant citations for all factual observations; the quantity difference and missing supplied acceptance record remain visible; no invented resolution or legal conclusion. Alternative observations are welcome if their sources and limits are explicit.

A proposed change to this reference must explain the affected observation and citation. Track the pack version/commit with each evaluation. No efficiency gain is claimed before an external comparison is performed.

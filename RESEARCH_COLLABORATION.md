# Research collaboration and work already delivered

**GREYC · UNIVERSITÉ DE CAEN NORMANDIE · 2025–2026**

OpenProof is led by **Gersende Ryard de Parcey**, founder of TruthX. Research work has already been carried out with **Professor [Gaël Dias](https://dias.users.greyc.fr/)** at GREYC and a University of Caen Normandie student team.

The collaboration began in **2025** and continued in **2026**. A research collaboration agreement was signed in 2025. The 2025–2026 student project delivered a report, a presentation and source code in May 2026.

## What the team explored and implemented
The delivered material describes a local-LLM prototype organised with LangGraph, covering the following research areas:

| Area | Work described in the delivered material |
| --- | --- |
| Document preparation | OCR/text extraction, identification of actors and generation of synthetic test cases |
| Consistency and uncertainty | Experimental chronological/logic checks and examination of speculative hypotheses |
| Language and competing accounts | Experimental analysis of language, actors' accounts and discrepancies |
| Structured output | Assembly of an intermediate JSON representation and model-based evaluation loops |
| Report generation | Reports adapted to different readers and readable output files |

**Thirteen modules were designed; this is not a claim that exactly thirteen finished agents were delivered.** The report explains that some modules were split across several agents and others were not implemented. The presentation identifies RAG, OSINT, the media-noise index and the immutable final-dossier component as outside the delivered implementation.

## Delivered research and current product
- **Delivered in May 2026:** student research report, presentation and prototype source archive.
- **Research scope:** synthetic cases, local language models and experimental multi-agent workflows. The reported evaluation is research evidence to examine, not an independent certification of the product.
- **Current integration:** this student prototype has not yet been integrated into the current OpenProof engine. Its components must be assessed individually for reuse, interfaces and validation.
- **Public access:** this page acknowledges the work; it does not publish the student source archive, the private research documents or a new licence.
- **Product boundary:** experimental outputs do not establish intentions, legal admissibility, liability or truth. Integration requires evidence of usefulness and controls appropriate to the actual task.

A research foundation and team contributions therefore already exist. The [new public contributor circle](COMMUNITY.md) is intended to extend this work through defined tasks, not to present OpenProof as starting from nothing.

Case brief

Fictional educational material. Editorial companion to the public RPO example; not generated by the private Legal application, signed, independently registered or professionally validated.

The question

Was the final ventilation test completed before the building was handed over, and what document establishes the timing?

What the example records

ReferenceRecorded informationLimit
ev-001 · Contract requirementA final ventilation test is required before handover.No contract original is supplied.
ev-002 · 15 July meeting minutesThe building is declared ready for handover.The declaration does not itself demonstrate test completion.
ev-003 · 16 July inspectionThe final airflow test is recorded as outstanding.The example does not establish later completion or the actual handover date.

The discrepancy

The readiness declaration and the inspection finding need to be reconciled against the contract requirement. The record preserves all three references. It does not resolve the discrepancy by deleting one account or treating it as a proven falsehood.

Questions for human review

  1. Obtain and examine the final test report, if it exists.
  2. Confirm the actual handover date and the applicable requirement.
  3. Check the scope, authorship and versions of the meeting and inspection records.
  4. Record any remaining disagreement or missing material before drawing a conclusion.

What remains unestablished

The underlying events, source authenticity, intent, causation and liability have not been established by this demonstration. Source hashes and the PDF field in its JSON are placeholders. The public comparison checks a record against a reference; it does not verify the underlying documents.

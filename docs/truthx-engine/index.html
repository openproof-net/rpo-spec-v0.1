01 · DEFINITION

A proof record should not begin when the conflict does.

TruthX does not declare an absolute truth. It organises the conditions required to determine what sources support, what remains contested, what is missing and whether execution remains coherent with recorded obligations and decisions.

The same engine can reconstruct a situation after failure or maintain integrity while a project is still moving. The probative record becomes a natural output of responsible execution rather than an emergency reconstruction years later.

Public boundary. This page explains the purpose, operating model and guarantees. The deterministic rules, orchestration, thresholds and private implementation remain confidential.

02 · TWO OPERATING MODES

One engine. Two moments of intervention.

Choose the moment. The integrity discipline remains the same.

Reconstruct what happened without rewriting history.

TruthX structures fragmented sources, competing narratives, chronology, effective powers, contradictions, hypotheses and causal mechanisms. Human reviewers decide what can enter the reviewed record.

  1. Collect and preserve sources
  2. Separate statements from established events
  3. Expose contradictions, gaps and alternatives
  4. Produce a reviewed, verifiable basis for audit, mediation or legal work
Legal EvidenceInvestigationAuditInsurance claimsMediation

Keep execution demonstrable while it is still in motion.

TruthX compares obligations, milestones, declarations, decisions, actions, corrections and evidence of delivery. It identifies incoherence early enough for a responsible human to act.

  1. Connect project and operational systems
  2. Test declared progress against evidence and acceptance criteria
  3. Preserve decisions, dissent and accepted risks
  4. Generate successive RPO snapshots as the situation evolves

03 · INTEGRITY CONTROLS

TruthX pilots verifiability — not the professional work itself.

Each control asks whether a critical representation can be demonstrated and challenged.

01Declarations

What was asserted, by whom, when, from which source and with which limits?

02Milestones

What was expected, by which date and according to which acceptance criteria?

03Decisions

Who decided, using which information, options, validations and accepted risks?

04Responsibilities

Who was required to act, who could actually act and who had the power to block or correct?

05Corrections

What remediation was requested, promised, executed, verified, refused or left unanswered?

06Outcome

What was ultimately delivered, and which evidence supports comparison with the original expectation?

TruthX does not manage the work on behalf of professionals. It ensures that what is declared, decided, changed, corrected and delivered remains demonstrable.

04 · FROM CORRECTION TO CONTENTION

The record serves prevention first. Litigation is the last possible use.

Detection→Correction→Arbitration→Audit→Insurance→Mediation→Defence→Litigation

A single reviewed basis can support early remediation, governance, claims handling and — only if necessary — a defensible dispute record.

08 · RESEARCH LANDSCAPE

The components exist. The complete category remains fragmented.

TruthX is informed by adjacent disciplines without claiming to have invented traceability, assurance or provenance.

Operational ontology and digital threads

Platforms already connect enterprise objects, actions and systems. TruthX adds a native discipline for contested knowledge, competing hypotheses and probative review.

Project records and progressive assurance

Construction and infrastructure tools already preserve documents, requirements and compliance evidence. TruthX aims to compare what systems declare with what sources can actually demonstrate.

Investigation and legal reconstruction

eDiscovery and investigation platforms reconstruct facts after the event. TruthX combines that retrospective capability with integrity controls while execution is still underway.

Publicly defensible position

We have not identified a public product unifying retrospective reconstruction, continuous integrity, explicit epistemic states, effective power, competing causal hypotheses, OSINT, human review and portable RPO snapshots as one native architecture.

This is a public product landscape statement, not a patentability opinion or exhaustive freedom-to-operate analysis.

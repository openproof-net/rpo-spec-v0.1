01 · DEFINITION

What the RPO guarantees.
And what it does not claim to guarantee.

An RPO makes any change to the sealed core detectable. It preserves the provenance, transformations and reservations required for independent review.

Essential boundary: object integrity does not prove content truth. OpenProof structures and makes records verifiable; it does not adjudicate.

01 · DEFINITION

What the RPO guarantees.
And what it does not claim to guarantee.

An RPO makes any change to the sealed core detectable. It preserves evidence provenance, transformations and explicit reservations required for independent review.

Essential boundary: object integrity does not prove content truth. OpenProof structures and makes records verifiable; it does not adjudicate.

  1. 01
    Fragmented

    Documents, emails, reports, declarations and project systems contain different parts of the situation.

    Risk: the record is reconstructed too late, from competing versions.
  2. 02
    Structured

    Sources, actors, events, decisions and evidence references are represented in one stable state.

    Result: decision traceability becomes inspectable rather than merely asserted.
  3. 03
    Qualified

    Contradictions, missing material, reservations and unresolved points remain visible.

    Result: audit-ready does not mean uncertainty-free.
  4. 04
    Verifiable

    A deterministic representation makes every copy independently comparable.

    Result: silent alteration becomes detectable without trusting OpenProof.

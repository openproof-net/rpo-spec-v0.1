01 · WHAT SHE SEES

ORGANISATIONS DO NOT LACK DATA. THEY LACK A COMMON, VERIFIABLE REPRESENTATION OF REALITY.

Gersende steps in when several versions of the same situation coexist without being properly comparable. Her work makes visible the gaps between formal structure, effective power, decisions, execution and outcome.

01 · POWER

Formal organisation / actual operation

Separate displayed roles from the access, dependencies, capacity to act and blocking power that can actually be observed.

02 · INFORMATION

Available data / shareable reality

Connect fragmented sources, provenance, versions and contradictions without turning their mere presence into truth.

03 · DECISION

Recorded decision / real mechanism

Reconstruct who decided, with which information, disagreements, constraints and accepted risks.

04 · EXECUTION

Declared progress / demonstrated outcome

Compare what was expected, announced, corrected and ultimately delivered against explicit criteria and preserved evidence.

02 · WHAT SHE BUILT

A UNIVERSAL FACTUAL, DECISION AND PROBATIVE INTEGRITY ENGINE.

TruthX Engine does not declare truth and does not replace any authorised professional. It organises the conditions required to determine what the sources support, what remains contested, what is missing and whether execution remains consistent with recorded obligations and decisions.

01 · FACTUAL

Factual integrity

Assertions, events, contradictions and unknowns remain connected to their sources and degree of support.

02 · DECISION

Decision integrity

Decisions preserve their question, available information, options, validations, disagreements and accepted risks.

03 · PROBATIVE

Probative integrity

Declarations, actions, corrections and outcomes remain demonstrable, versioned, contestable and auditable.

TruthX does not manage the work on behalf of professionals. It ensures that what is declared, decided, changed, corrected and delivered remains demonstrable.

03 · TWO OPERATING MODES

ONE ENGINE. TWO MOMENTS OF INTERVENTION.

The moment changes, not the discipline. TruthX can reconstruct a situation after failure or maintain its integrity while action can still be corrected.

01 · AFTER THE EVENT

Retrospective reconstruction

When the crisis, dispute or failure already exists, TruthX reconstructs the real system without rewriting history.

  • Sources and chronology
  • Formal and effective power
  • Contradictions and gaps
  • Competing hypotheses and causalities
  • Reviewed basis for audit, mediation or legal work
02 · DURING EXECUTION

Continuous integrity

While a project is still in motion, TruthX controls continuity across obligations, decisions, actions, corrections and delivery evidence.

  • Requirements, milestones and dependencies
  • Decisions, disagreements and accepted risks
  • Declared progress against acceptance criteria
  • Alerts and evidence requests
  • Successive RPO snapshots

05 · THE ORIGIN

FACTS CAN EXIST AND STILL LOSE THEIR FORCE.

Truth is not enough. It must withstand the power of narrative.

Two ruptures — one institutional, one in governance — revealed the same failure: facts can be real, evidence can be preserved and responsibilities can exist without remaining intelligible or demonstrable. The TruthX doctrine emerged from this structural repetition, not from a desire to turn a personal history into a commercial argument.

01Institutional rupture

Understanding that procedures designed to protect can separate responsibility, information and the actual power to act.

02Governance rupture

Understanding that preserving documents is not enough when their chronology, provenance, relationships and contradictions have not been reconstructed.

Gersende de Parcey · Founder of OpenProof

Truth is not enough.
It must withstand the power of narrative.

When facts lose their structure, power returns to whoever controls the narrative. OpenProof was created to preserve their provenance, their relationships and the way a conclusion was constructed.

THE ORIGIN

Two ruptures.
The same mechanism.

OpenProof grew from a repeated experience: seeing real facts lose their force when they enter institutional, legal or organisational systems capable of fragmenting, isolating or burying them beneath a better-prepared narrative.

After the death of her husband, an army officer, Gersende de Parcey encountered a rarely discussed form of institutional violence: procedures designed in the name of protection that can nevertheless remove a responsible adult’s ability to act, decide and protect her own children.

Years later, a crisis involving governance, responsibility, accusations and competing narratives created a second turning point. The evidence existed and had been preserved. Yet possessing it was not enough: its chronology, provenance, relationships, contradictions and underlying mechanisms still had to be reconstructed.

Two different ruptures revealed the same mechanism: facts can exist and still lose the battle when they lack structure.

No person or organisation should depend solely on their ability to explain, under pressure, what happened to them.

TWENTY YEARS IN THE FIELD

Reconstruct the real system.

Every organisation has two realities: the one it describes, and the one that actually drives decisions, behaviour and performance.

For more than twenty years, Gersende de Parcey has worked alongside leaders whose organisations had become difficult to read: fragmented information, blurred accountability, slowed decisions, silos, hidden costs and invisible power dynamics.

Her work does not begin with a standard solution. It begins with simple questions, applied rigorously:

  • Who actually makes decisions?
  • Who actually creates value?
  • Where does information originate, who observes it, who transforms it and who transmits it?
  • When does an assumption become a belief, then a decision?
  • Which causal chains connect that decision to its consequences?

She reconstructs information, decision, accountability and execution flows. She separates formal roles from real roles, symptoms from causes, claims from sources and apparent visibility from actual operation.

This practice can be described as a form of Organizational Reality Engineering: making the real operation of an organisation readable enough to be understood, challenged and transformed.

THE LIMIT

Seeing quickly was not enough.

For twenty years, this method relied on experience, observation, interviews, cross-checking and manual reconstruction.

The diagnosis could emerge quickly. Making it demonstrable took time: retrieving sources, rebuilding sequences, separating observed facts from reported information and documenting every causal link.

The problem was not only to understand. It was to show how that understanding had been constructed.

THE SHIFT

AI made the method reproducible.

Artificial intelligence does not guarantee truth. Data can be incomplete, sources contested and models fallible.

But it makes a new capability possible: structuring, connecting and examining volumes of information that could not previously be reconstructed manually with the same depth.

Technology does not replace human judgment. It can give judgment memory, structure, provenance and traceability.

TruthX Engine emerged from this convergence between a field-tested method and a new technological capability.

WHAT SHE IS BUILDING

A method transformed into an engine, infrastructure and verifiable object.

01 · METHOD TRUTHX ENGINE

The deterministic structuring engine. It connects sources, facts, decisions, actors, contradictions and consequences under explicit rules.

02 · INFRASTRUCTURE OPENPROOF

The probative infrastructure. It makes the construction of reasoning inspectable, traceable, explainable and auditable.

03 · RESULT RPO

The Registered Probative Object. A registered probative object preserving sources, transformations, reservations and integrity data.

OpenProof is the probative infrastructure. TruthX Engine is the deterministic structuring engine powering it. RPO is the Registered Probative Object it produces.

FOUNDING CONVICTION

Understanding is not enough. It must be demonstrable.

OpenProof does not declare what is true. It makes verifiable how a representation of facts was constructed: from which sources, under which rules, through which transformations, with which contradictions and uncertainties, and under whose responsibility.

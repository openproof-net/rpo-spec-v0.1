02 · SEE AN RPO · FICTIONAL DATA

WHAT DOES AN RPO ACTUALLY SHOW?START WITH THE SITUATION, NOT THE CODE.

An RPO is neither “the truth” nor a legal conclusion. It is a structured record preserving what was expected, what was declared, what the sources show and what remains unresolved.

In one sentence: the RPO keeps the situation, sources, limits and change-detection fingerprint together.

01 · THE SITUATION

FOUR ELEMENTS ARE ENOUGH TO UNDERSTAND THE GAP.

This example is entirely fictional. It shows the RPO logic without exposing a real case.

01 · EXPECTED

Before handover

The contract requires the final ventilation test to be completed before the building is handed over.

02 · DECLARED

Ready for handover

The 15 July meeting minutes state that the building is ready for handover.

03 · SOURCE

Test still outstanding

The 16 July inspection report states that the final airflow test has not yet been completed.

04 · UNRESOLVED

A gap must be addressed

The RPO does not automatically decide liability. It preserves the gap, the sources and the question that remains.

02 · WHY IT MATTERS

THE RPO DOES NOT REPLACE THE WORK. IT PREVENTS THE WORK FROM BECOMING INDEMONSTRABLE.

01 · SAME RECORD

One shared representation

The teams, auditor, insurer or lawyer can start from the same recorded state rather than reconstructing competing versions later.

02 · NO SILENT REWRITE

Every change becomes visible

Changing one word, date or source produces a different fingerprint. The earlier state cannot disappear silently.

03 · EARLIER ACTION

Correct before conflict

The gap can be discussed, checked and corrected while it is still repairable — before audit, mediation or litigation.

03 · WHAT THE OBJECT PRESERVES

ONE SITUATION, READABLE BY PEOPLE AND STRUCTURED FOR MACHINES.

01 · CONTEXT

What is this about?

Version, identifier, date, issuer and the subject concerned.

02 · SOURCES

What supports the record?

References, descriptions, identifiers and declared fingerprints of the material used.

03 · REPRESENTATION

What can be said now?

A structured summary that preserves the unresolved point and does not turn a hypothesis into a fact.

04 · INTEGRITY

Has the file changed?

A reproducible digital fingerprint makes two copies of the same object comparable.

04 · TWO READINGS

PEOPLE READ THE MEANING. MACHINES READ THE STRUCTURE.

The same object can be presented simply without losing its technical format.

Human view

Human reading. The building was declared ready while the final ventilation test was still outstanding.

The record preserves the contractual requirement, the progress declaration, the inspection report and the question that remains open.

It does not automatically state that someone lied or that legal liability has been established.
